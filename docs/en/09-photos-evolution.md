## What the photos tell, in order

1. First, getting the games working (PS1 in PBP, GBA hacks) and finding a way to use the timer.
2. Then the timer itself: test screens, the on-screen alert, and the fight against flicker.
3. In parallel, the PS1 graphics comparison (which I still need to redo properly).
4. Last, the menu: history, settings and the button to wipe it.
